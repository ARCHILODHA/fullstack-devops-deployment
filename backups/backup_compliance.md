# Backup Compliance

## Purpose

This document defines compliance requirements for backups to ensure that backup operations meet organizational, security, and regulatory standards.

## Compliance Requirements

- Backups must be protected from unauthorized access.
- Sensitive backup data must be encrypted.
- Backup retention periods must be documented.
- Restore procedures must be tested regularly.
- Backup activities must generate audit logs.
- Access to backup storage must follow least-privilege principles.

## Data Classification

Backup policies should depend on the sensitivity of the data:

| Data Type | Protection Level | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Standard | 30 days |
| Internal | Medium | 90 days |
| Confidential | High | 180 days |
| Critical | Very High | 1 year |

## Audit

Backup configurations and access permissions should be reviewed periodically.

## Review Frequency

Compliance requirements should be reviewed at least once every six months.
