# Auto Scaling

## Definition

Auto Scaling automatically increases or decreases computing resources based on demand.

## Advantages

- Cost optimization
- Better performance
- High availability
- Handles traffic spikes

## Scaling Types

### Horizontal Scaling
Add more servers.

### Vertical Scaling
Increase CPU or RAM of existing server.

## Example

Traffic ↑

2 Servers → 4 Servers → 8 Servers

Traffic ↓

8 Servers → 4 Servers → 2 Servers
