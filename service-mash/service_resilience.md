# Service Resilience

## Introduction

Service Resilience refers to a system's ability to continue functioning despite failures.

---

## Techniques

- Retry
- Timeout
- Circuit Breaker
- Load Balancing
- Health Checks

---

## Benefits

- High Availability
- Better User Experience
- Reduced Downtime
- Faster Recovery

---

## Service Mesh Features

Modern Service Mesh platforms provide resilience automatically using Envoy sidecar proxies.

Examples:

- Istio
- Linkerd
- Kuma
